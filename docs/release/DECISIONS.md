# Decisions that block the professional-verification release

This file lists only the information and choices that must come from the owner (or the lawyer). Everything else is done or in progress, and the release plan is in `RELEASE_PLAN.md`.

Each decision has a recommendation and the reason for it. The placeholders (`{{…}}`) are the ones used in `docs/legal/DRAFT_privacy-professional-verification.md` and `DRAFT_terms-professional-verification.md`.

| # | Decision | Recommendation | Why | Blocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Controller** `{{CONTROLLER}}`: legal name, address, VAT or company number | — (owner's data) | The privacy notice must name who is responsible; today it says only "Phygo" | Privacy text |
| D2 | **Data-protection contact** `{{PRIVACY_CONTACT}}` | A **dedicated, monitored address** (for example a privacy@ alias on the PHYGO domain) | Requests have legal deadlines (one month). A dedicated address keeps them out of general mail and can be handed over later | Privacy text |
| D3 | **Who reviews documents** `{{ADMINS}}` | **The owner only** for now, named by role ("the PHYGO owner, a physiotherapist"). For the owner's own account: check the official public register and record the result like any other request | Fewer people with access to identity documents means less risk. The register check is public and verifiable by anyone, which limits the self-review concern. Add a second reviewer when the volume requires it | Privacy text, `PHYGO_ADMIN_EMAILS` stays as is |
| D4 | **Legal basis** `{{LEGAL_BASIS}}` (with the lawyer) | **Contract** (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) for checking eligibility, plus **legitimate interest** (Art. 6(1)(f)) for keeping the decision record and preventing abuse | The Terms (§3) reserve the clinical functions to qualified professionals, so the check is part of the service. Keeping the record after a revocation, or deleting an account, is not "necessary for the contract", so it needs a second basis with a balancing test | Privacy text |
| D5 | **Vercel plan** | **Pro** | Vercel's documentation limits Hobby to personal, non-commercial use, and the DPA text found covers Pro and Enterprise. PHYGO has paid plans and processes professionals' documents and health data | Applicable DPA, privacy section 6 |

Save every sub-processor list, with dates | Section 6 of the current privacy notice already says every provider is "bound by a Data Processing Agreement": that must be true | Privacy text, release |
