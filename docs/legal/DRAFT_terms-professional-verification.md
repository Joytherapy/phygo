# DRAFT — Terms of Service, new section "Professional verification"

**Status: unpublished draft, for legal review. Do not copy it into `app/terms/page.tsx` until it has been reviewed and the owner has approved it.**

## Where it goes and why
- **Placement:** a new section after §3 "Eligibility", numbered here as 3A.
- **Link to the declaration:** the verification form already says that false statements may lead to suspension "within the limits of the Terms of Service". Today the Terms do not cover this, so this section fills the gap.
- **Relation to existing sections:**
  - §10 "Termination" already allows suspension for violations, "with notice where reasonably possible";
  - §9 already keeps the liability that cannot be limited by law.

  This section adds gradual, proportionate steps before closing an account, and does **not** exclude PHYGO's liability.

## Points for the lawyer `[LEGAL REVIEW]`
1. Paragraph (a), last sentence: verification "is not an assessment of clinical competence". It describes what the check covers; confirm it is not read as a limitation of liability.
2. Paragraph (b): the duty to report a suspension or removal from the register. Confirm it is proportionate and enforceable.
3. Paragraph (c):
   - the graduated measures;
   - the "urgent cases" exception (patient safety, unlawful use);
   - whether to mention reporting to the competent authorities or the Order.

   That last point is not included; it is a decision for the owner and the lawyer.
4. Paragraph (d): "your data stays viewable" is true today (read access is kept). **Export:** confirm whether an export function exists or will exist before mentioning it.
5. Paragraph (e): the 60-day transition and how it is notified (in-app today; e-mail not implemented).
6. Consumer and professional law: users are professionals. Confirm the governing law (§12 still says "[jurisdiction to be confirmed]").
7. The four language versions must stay aligned. State which version prevails, if any.

  - documenti alterati;
  - l'uso non autorizzato di dati o credenziali altrui;

**(e) Account verificati prima dell'introduzione del documento.**
- Questi account devono inviare il documento entro 60 giorni dalla comunicazione.
- Se nome, numero e Ordine restano invariati, lo stato di verifica non cambia durante il controllo.
- Se la scadenza passa senza un documento confermato, le funzioni che richiedono la verifica sono sospese fino alla conferma. Nessun dato viene cancellato.

**(f) I tuoi diritti di legge.** Questa sezione non limita i diritti che la legge ti riconosce, né la responsabilità di PHYGO nei casi in cui la legge non consente di limitarla.

## English

### 3A. Professional verification
**(a) Reserved features and what verification confirms.**
- Some PHYGO features are reserved for physiotherapists whose registration PHYGO has verified:
  - AI applied to real clinical information;
  - video consultations;
  - managing real patients.
- Verification is carried out by a person. It compares the details you declare, the document you provide and the official professional register.
- Verification only confirms that, on the date of the check, the declared registration was listed and matched. It is not an assessment of your clinical competence.

**(b) Your commitments.** You agree to:
- provide true, up-to-date details that refer to you;
- upload only authentic, unaltered documents that you are entitled to use;
- not use other people's data, documents or credentials;
- tell us without delay if your registration is suspended, removed or changed.

**(c) Measures if problems arise.**
- This clause applies if any of the following emerges:
  - false statements;
  - altered documents;
  - unauthorised use of other people's data or credentials;
  - a mismatch with the register.
- In these cases PHYGO may take the following measures, in proportion to the seriousness of the case and after telling you the reason:
  1. ask you for information or a new document;
  2. temporarily restrict the features that need verification while it checks;
  3. reject the request or revoke the verification;
  4. in serious or repeated cases, suspend or close the account under section 10.
- Except in urgent cases involving patient safety or unlawful use, you can respond before a revocation, and you can always submit a new request.

**(d) Your data stays.** Restriction or revocation does not delete your data or your patients' data, which remain viewable. Data is handled under the Privacy Policy and section 5.

**(e) Accounts verified before documents were required.**
- These accounts must send the document within 60 days of notice.
- If your name, number and Order stay the same, your verification status does not change during the check.
- If the deadline passes without a confirmed document, the features that need verification are suspended until it is confirmed. No data is deleted.

**(f) Your legal rights.** This section does not limit the rights the law gives you, nor PHYGO's liability where the law does not allow it to be limited.
