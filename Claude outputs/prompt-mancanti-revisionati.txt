=== PROMPT RIVISTI — 4 immagini bloccate dal filtro nudità ===
Incolla sempre PRIMA lo "stile base" che hai già (quello con TITLE/MAIN ILLUSTRATION/BOTTOM PANELS/FOOTER), poi incolla uno di questi 4 blocchi.
Modifiche fatte rispetto all'originale: via ogni riferimento a genitali esterni/pose "lithotomy", tutto ricondotto a diagramma osseo/muscolare interno o schema astratto, zero rendering di superficie corporea esterna.

1) UROGENITAL DIAPHRAGM (urogenital-diaphragm.png)

TITLE: "UROGENITAL" (white) "DIAPHRAGM" (cyan).
SUBTITLE: EXTERNAL URETHRAL SPHINCTER • MID-URETHRA SUPPORT • DEEP TRANSVERSE PERINEI
MAIN: full midsagittal female pelvis internal X-ray glass render (same crop as a standard sagittal anatomy plate, not a close-up genital crop), the triangular urogenital diaphragm highlighted bright cyan as an internal fibromuscular sheet spanning between the ischiopubic rami, positioned just below the dimmer levator ani. Render as translucent internal tissue layers only, no external body surface or skin visible — purely internal skeletal/muscular structures on a dark background. Label Pubic Symphysis, Bladder, Urethra, Vagina, Levator Ani, External Urethral Sphincter.
PANEL (1): MID-URETHRA SUPPORT — small labeled diagram showing how this internal layer supports the middle third of the urethra, independent of the levator ani above it; one-sentence clinical note on stress urinary incontinence when deficient.

2) PUBOCERVICAL FASCIA (pubocervical-fascia.png)

TITLE: "PUBOCERVICAL" (white) "FASCIA" (cyan).
SUBTITLE: ANTERIOR COMPARTMENT • BLADDER SUPPORT • CYSTOCELE
MAIN: full midsagittal internal X-ray glass render of the female pelvis (standard sagittal anatomy plate framing, same as other fascia images in this set), the pubocervical fascia highlighted bright cyan as an internal mesh sheet between pubic bone and cervix, behind the bladder and in front of the vagina — all rendered as translucent internal glass-tissue layers, no external body surface. Label Pubic Symphysis, Bladder, Cervix, Vagina, Urethra.
PANEL (1): CYSTOCELE MECHANISM — small before/after diagram showing loss of the rigid anterior support and the resulting bladder descent; one-sentence note on the stress urinary incontinence link (loss of the urethra's rigid backboard).

3) ILIOINGUINAL NERVE (ilioinguinal-nerve.png)

TITLE: "ILIOINGUINAL" (white) "NERVE" (cyan).
SUBTITLE: L1 NERVE ROOT • ABDOMINAL WALL • INGUINAL NEURALGIA
MAIN: anterior abdominal wall internal X-ray glass render (bony pelvis + muscle layers only, no external skin or genital surface depicted), the nerve highlighted as a bright cyan glowing line from its L1 origin running anterolaterally through the abdominal wall muscle layers to exit the superficial inguinal ring. Its cutaneous sensory territory is shown only as a small pale shaded zone on the lower abdominal wall/upper medial thigh region of the internal diagram, not as external body surface. Label L1 Nerve Root, Iliac Crest, Inguinal Canal, Superficial Inguinal Ring, Pubic Tubercle.
PANEL (1): POST-SURGICAL ENTRAPMENT — one-sentence note on injury during inguinal hernia repair, C-section or pelvic surgery causing ilioinguinal neuralgia in its distribution.
